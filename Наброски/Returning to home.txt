I was returning from the war
To my sweet home.
From 'eathen's land, from distant shore,
From ten-years roam.
I reached the green and neaty hills
Of father's land.
But in my sack like burden stils
The muddy sand.

My native forests and my oaks
They waits for me.
There is my town and my folks,
But what I see?!
