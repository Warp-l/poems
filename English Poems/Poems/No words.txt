No Words.

My friends. I know that we can hurt the outer world.
It's sick. It makes us weary, numb and cold.
And even if we try to be poetic,
Our words seem shy, distrustful and pathetic.

The outer world belongs to only stars.
There are no silver rhymes or bards' guitars.
The vacuum swallows every human sound.
No words. There's only silence all around.
