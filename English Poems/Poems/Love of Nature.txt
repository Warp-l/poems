Love of Nature.

The light of stars, the eyes of storms
Could never be described.
And how could such unstable forms
Be still and moderated?
The flaming spheres, the roaring winds,
The rage of Nature's will,
What force forbids their leaving their pins
And makes them all stand still.
Like a teacher or the angry dad
With rod in his strong hand
Can't hit his own beloved lad
And can only pretend.
And in the world, which is so great
And loves us without doubt
Lord gives us chances to stay straight
And doesn't chastise, but loves.
