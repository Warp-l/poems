Before me.

I was returning from the war
To my sweet home.
From 'eathen's land, from distant shore,
From ten years' roam.
I reached the green and pretty hills
Of father's land.
But in my sack like a burden stills
The muddy sand.
My native forests and the swamps
They wait for me.
There are my folks, there is my town,
But what d' I see?
The charred ruins like a scree
And nothing more
The war has walked before me
From distant shore.
