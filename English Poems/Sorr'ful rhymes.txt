Sorr'ful Rhymes.

There was a little man.
The man was very foolish.
And he served a lady
She was so proud 'n' selfish.

And she used 'is soul
Like a personal clown.
'e didn't care at all
'e bowed to 'er crown.

But the time's gone by
He grew self-assured.
'is soul forgot her cry.
'e was finally cured.

He b'came a bartender
Of his sorr'ful rhymes.
Now he'll not surrender
To that queenie times.
