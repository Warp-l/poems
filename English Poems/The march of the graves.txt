The March of the Graves.

When the time is gone.
When the soldier is born.
When no lyric says -
Then the colonel shows the ways.

Then the army marches.
Then the flags on arches.
Then the anthem call
And drafts to the army us all.

And we walk from home.
And we hold the crown.
And the distant lands
Will fall 'nto imperial hands.

Will the baldrick be white?
Will the anthems be right?
Will we march the world
For empire at every hole?

For the honour'd death.
For an empire's wraith.
For a queenie grace.
All army will fall in graves.
