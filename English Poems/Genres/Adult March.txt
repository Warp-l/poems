Adult March.

When we were young, all songs were sung
And nothing left to sing.
We try to cry with all our lungs,
But our voices do not ring.
We're building rhymes, we hear the chimes,
But can't refrain them all.
Like hiding evidence of crimes,
We hide our voices in a hole.
Our adult march is straight like larch
It's simple and very clear.
It's tasteless like a powdered starch -
Just structure, not a feel.
