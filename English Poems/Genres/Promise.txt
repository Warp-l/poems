Promise.

We will try to keep our promises.
Not right now but later.
We won't listen the silly advices,
How to make poetry.
We will roam in the quest of rhythms
Like the Flying Dutchman.
And no science or algorithms
Could achieve the judgment.
We'll remember our early voices,
Our simple lyrics.
We'll not stay in pretentious poses
Like a cathedral clerics.
We will find our only freedom,
Our only aches.
We'll not try to find ancient wisdom.
Make our own mistakes.
