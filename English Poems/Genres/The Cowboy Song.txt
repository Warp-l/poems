The Cowboy Song.

The long devil's tongue of my colt
Is dangerous and precise.
You could only feel the darkening jolt,
When fate has thrown her dice.
The war bonnet or the cowboy hat
Can't keep you from the grave.
When the bullets speak, when the bullets chat.
No diff, you craven or brave.

The prairies are my only sweet home
And I need nothing more.
My route leads me straight and alone
And lays from shore to shore.
My old Winchester tied to the saddle,
Shining bright like my horse's mane
Horse's hoofs click-clack like the trigger paddle
Carrying me across the plains.
