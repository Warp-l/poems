Fragility of beauty.

I bowed to a wild rose,
Staring at blooming grace.
She promised me to expose
Her petal-hidden face.

How could I ever suppose
Fragility of elegance.
I bowed to a withered rose
With mournful and tearful glance.

I thought about the thinning
Of life, of nature, of grace.
But in soul I feel... a beginning
Of sarcastic smile on my face.
