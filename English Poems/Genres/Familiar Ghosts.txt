Familiar Ghosts.

In the sleepless night,
When thoughts are tight
I was sitting beside the fire.
Familiar ghosts
From the distant coasts,
They ignited my soul like the pyre.
My aulden friend,
He was descended
From heaven right to hell.
And now he calls
From whence he falls.
He waits for me there as well.

I tried to pray,
I tried to stay
Confident in my soldier's will,
But in a gleam
Came to my dream
All soldiers that I ever killed.
