The Soul's Shanty.

Around cape Horn, in fearful storm
Our crew is all aboard.
No matt'r what land the sailor came from -
He praises the only Lord.
   Chorus: We're praisin' only Lord!

Our clipper cries 'n' whines 'n' groans
Surrendin' to the deep,
No matt'r the year the sailor was born -
His life is short 'n' cheap.
   Chorus: Our lives are short 'n' cheap!

Our sails are torn, and clipper's mast
Is bowing in the wind.
But sailors' soul is hell-forged 'n' cast -
She overgrown with rind.
    Chorus: Our souls are cast in rind!

Some says we're fools, some says we're rude,
But only Lord can see -
That sailors' souls are hard-boil'd 'n' storm-brew'd
And bless'd by the sea.
   Chorus: We're blessed by the sea!
